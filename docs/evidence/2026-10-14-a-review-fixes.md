The generic quote checker still supports quotations crossing a cited paragraph boundary into its neighbour, as required by the analysis pipeline. A quotation entirely inside an uncited neighbour is rejected. Ambiguous hard-hyphen words remain a documented PDF prototype limitation; the reader does not claim universal linguistic de-hyphenation.
